# Agent instructions

Read `TANA_SYSTEM.md` when present before inspecting or acting on a Tana workspace.
Treat it as shared domain guidance, not as proof of current Tana state. Verify relevant
structure through fresh reads, keep observations separate from interpretation, and obey
its write boundaries.

Use focused workflows under `skills/`. When no system exists yet, begin with
`skills/create-tana-system/SKILL.md`; do not invent or apply a schema without a complete
preview and explicit owner approval. Default to its Guided Discovery path unless the
owner requests Quick Start.
